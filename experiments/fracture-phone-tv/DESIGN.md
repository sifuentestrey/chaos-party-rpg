# Chaos Party interface direction

Reuse Fracture's verified art and animation. UI frames the shared world; it never replaces native scenery or paints invented props.

- Palette: dark jade/ink `#10181c`, raised panels `#1b292c`, warm paper `#eee8dc`, restrained gold `#e6bf7c`, subdued green `#91b9a2`, muted clay for blocked outcomes. Use contrast and spacing before adding another bordered card.
- Type: local Georgia for identity, section headings and shared narration; system sans-serif for input, controls and status. No external fonts or asset downloads.
- Phone: identity and health, prominent open action field and personal STOP, class signature, equipped gear and bag. Secondary wardrobe, saved looks, party travel and randomization preferences collapse. Preserve existing supported combinations and accurate native previews.
- Controls: minimum 44px touch targets; measured footer inset plus 20px; hide the footer while editing inputs/selects. Keep input and submit reachable when the visual viewport shrinks for the keyboard.
- TV: keep the native shared camera stationary within its scene. Small top phase/objective pill; at most one transient event caption near the bottom. Shared recap highlights at most three confirmed actions. Native combat effects and object collision/render mutations convey consequences.
- Voice: describe what the character can perceive and what actually happened. Keep connection failures factual and separate. Avoid parser/debug jargon, invented obstacles, fake success and command lists.
- Evidence: inspect at 1920×1080 and 1280×720, and phone widths 320/375/390/430. Use actual game state and recorded inputs; traces with private recovery URLs remain local and never enter commits/packages.

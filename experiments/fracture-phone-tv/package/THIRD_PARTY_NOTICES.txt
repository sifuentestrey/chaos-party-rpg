This kit supplies reconstructible adapter source and patch instructions, not
raw upstream game assets or databases. The launcher obtains the exact upstream source;
its LICENSE, authorship credits and source headers remain in that checkout.
Modified upstream files and adapter source remain available in the local clone
and experimental Chaos Party source. No proprietary replacement game is bundled.

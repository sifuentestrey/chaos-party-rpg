# Playing Chaos Party

Start the Mac launcher, put the host page on the TV, and scan its QR code from phones on the same Wi-Fi. Each phone creates a character. The TV is the shared world; phones are controllers. Keep the terminal open. No internet or AI is needed for the native adventure.

## The Crown Job

Start the heist. Investigate, talk or perform to find three clues; steal the crown from the chest and flee through the door. Players can also change the tavern: take, break, spill or extinguish displayed objects. Your first declaration locks; other players react; server dice decide the result. Rest to recover when hurt. A failed attempt does not grant its claimed outcome.

## The Dawn Bell

After victory, the host selects **Begin The Dawn Bell**. For a shorter outdoor session, the host may instead start the story normally and choose **Begin / resume The Dawn Bell** in host controls between turns. Existing characters and carried objects remain.

Copy the map from the tavern wall using the phone’s object controls, or declare **Read tavern map**. The discovered map persists and shows actual routes. The valley is bounded: tavern → clearing → ruins → woods → river → Bellweather → castle → cellar → bell vault. There are branches and two river crossings. Previously explored sandbox saves keep their old routes as well.

Travel requires a strict majority of connected players. The leader votes yes automatically; others choose **Help them** in the reaction window. Abstentions do not count as yes. Solo travel works. Ordinary actions need no vote.

Talk to the person at a location to activate their challenge. Use the displayed names/IDs to avoid ambiguity. Settle threats by befriending or fighting. To heal someone, take a cloth strip and **Bandage <person>**. To restore a broken object, **Repair <object>**. The bridge must be safe and repaired to cross; the ferryman provides the alternative. Help Bellweather’s mayor to gain castle passage. Repair the cellar door, then recover the Dawn Bell from its coffer and give it to the bellkeeper to reach the ending.

Use your own pockets: another player’s cloth or bell cannot be spent on your action. Players can give items to each other. Crafted objects retain their components; clever creations can carry a quest keepsake. Rewards are granted once. Damaged crossings, quest progress, NPC trust, inventory and discoveries persist across visits and saves.

The host can keep exploring after the Dawn Bell ending. A fresh heist resets story progress and scores, so use the existing confirmation intentionally. Campaigns autosave. Resume a saved room from the host and rejoin with the same phone; keep the private recovery PIN for a replacement phone.

## AI and acceptance

On the Mac, open host controls and choose **Dungeon Master setup**. Paste your Groq Free Plan connection key and confirm the Free Plan, then save. The key is stored privately outside the game package and is never sent to phones. No terminal commands are needed. Connect or disconnect between turns. The existing request limits are preserved.

Creative hosted AI remains optional and unqualified. Native supported commands remain available when it is absent, slow or limited; unrepresentable stunts may be rejected. This candidate has no claim of measured live-model quality or all-night free AI capacity. The 100/day and four/minute shared request guards remain intact.

Use a new test room first. Physical Mac/macOS, TV, audio, Wi-Fi, two real phones and group performance still require rehearsal. See TEST_BEFORE_GAME_NIGHT.md. Keep earlier installations and saves intact.
